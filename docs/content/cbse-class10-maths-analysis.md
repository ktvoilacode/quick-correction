Compiled 2026-10-27 from web sources. ## 1. Paper structure by year

All four years: 80 marks theory + 20 marks internal assessment = 100; duration 3 hours (10:30 to 13:30; 15 min reading time); 38 questions; Standard and Basic have the same skeleton.

| Item | 2023 | 2024 | 2025 | 2026 (Phase 1, 17 Feb) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sec A | 20 x 1 MCQ (reported) | 18 MCQ + 2 Assertion-Reason (AR) x 1 | 18 MCQ (Q1-18) + 2 AR (Q19-20) | 20 x 1 incl. 2 AR |
| Sec B | 5 x 2 VSA | 5 x 2 | 5 x 2 (Q21-25) | 5 x 2 |
| Sec C | 6 x 3 SA | 6 x 3 | 6 x 3 (Q26-31) | 6 x 3 |
| Sec D | 4 x 5 LA | 4 x 5 | 4 x 5 (Q32-35) | 4 x 5 |
| Sec E | 3 x 4 case-based | 3 x 4 | 3 x 4 (Q36-38, sub-parts 1+1+2) | 3 x 4 |
| Total | 38 Q / 80 | 38 / 80 | 38 / 80 | 38 / 80 |
| Internal choice | not found | 2 Q in each of B, C, D; 2-mark parts in E (2023-24 sample paper) | 2 Q each in B, C, D; all 2-mark parts in E | "in each section" (India TV, loose) |

### Competency / application vs recall
- Reported progression of the competency-based share for Class 10: about 30% (2021-22), 40% (2022-23), 50% from the 2024 boards; objective 20%, short/long answers 30% (https://english.madhyamam.com/lifestyle/education/cbse-revamps-assessment-scheme-for-10th-12th-for-2024-1147345, https://news.careers360.com/cbse-board-exam-2024-class-12-focus-on-mcqs-weigtage-for-short-and-long-answer-questions-reduced/amp, https://news.aglasem.com/competency-based-questions-read-cbses-official-circular-on-new-exam-pattern-for-class-9-10-11-12). Other blogs quote 30% or 40% competency (Careers360/Vedantu per a search summary), so the figure is contested; 50% matches the most-cited news and a 2024-25 circular (https://www.cbseacademic.nic.in/web_material/Circulars/2024/30_Circular_2024.pdf appears in search results but could not be opened).
- Cognitive typology (2025 blueprint, Careers360, Dec 2024): Standard = 43 marks remember/understand (54%), 19 apply (24%), 18 analyse/evaluate/create (22%). Basic = 60 (75%), 12 (15%), 8 (10%). So "application + higher order" is about 46% in Standard and 25% in Basic. https://school.careers360.com/articles/cbse-class-10-mathematics-blueprint-2025-chapter-wise-weightage-exam-pattern. PW's 2025-26 weightage page quotes the Basic split (60/12/8) only (https://www.pw.live/school-prep/exams/cbse-class-10-maths-chapter-wise-weightage-2025-26).
- 2026: reports say about 70% of Standard and about 60% of Basic questions came straight from NCERT exercises (https://news.careers360.com/cbse-2026-class-10-12-board-exam-live-maths-biotechnology-question-paper-analysis-answer-key-admit-card-download-school-updates), and MCQs were "concept-based/lengthy" rather than formula-based (prosyllabus above).

## 3. Weightage: official units vs observed

Official unit marks (80), consistent in most sources (https://school.careers360.com/articles/cbse-class-10-mathematics-blueprint-2025-chapter-wise-weightage-exam-pattern, https://www.pw.live/school-prep/exams/...): Number Systems 6, Algebra 20, Coordinate Geometry 6, Geometry 15, Trigonometry 12, Mensuration 10, Statistics & Probability 11. DISAGREEMENT: PW's 2025-26 page lists Number Systems 5 and Algebra 21.

Chapter-level marks (Careers360 2023 "marking scheme" table; blog-level estimate, not official): Real Numbers 6; Polynomials 4, Pair of Linear Eq 3, Quadratic 9, AP 4; Coordinate Geometry 6; Triangles 9, Circles 6; Intro Trig 4, Trig Identities 4, Heights & Distances 4; Areas Related to Circles 4, Surface Areas & Volumes 6; Statistics 7, Probability 4. Sums to 80. Quadratic Equations at 9 looks high versus the typical Algebra spread; treat chapter splits as approximate.

Observed (qualitative only), 2026: topics named across sources: Real Numbers (HCF/LCM, irrationality), AP (MCQ and Section E case), Probability (MCQ, 2-mark, dice sums), Circles (tangents, cyclic quadrilaterals; tricky), Triangles (BPT proof), Pair of Linear Equations, Statistics (median/mode, mean with two unknowns), SAV (cylinder with hemispherical ends), Trigonometry application (Section E case) (https://www.indiatvnews.com/education/news/cbse-10th-maths-analysis-2026-paper-was-well-balanced-ncert-focused-check-students-experts-reactions-2026-02-17-1030623 ; prosyllabus; Careers360 above). Section E 2026 case topics reported: AP, surface area and volume, application of trigonometry (India TV; Careers360 mentions AP for Basic). 2025: only generic reports (coordinate geometry Q25 and trig 3 and 5 mark questions said to be tricky). 2024: Alka Kapur said case studies were "not from expected chapters"; no chapter list. 2023: no chapter list.

High-yield (consistent in blueprint and every analysis): Algebra (Quadratic, AP, PLE), Trigonometry, Triangles, Statistics, Surface Areas & Volumes, Probability. Lower frequency: Real Numbers (single block), Coordinate Geometry (6 marks, 2-3 questions), Areas Related to Circles. I found no source claiming any chapter is "rarely asked"; the blueprint gives every chapter at least 3-4 marks.

## 4. Per-chapter question styles

Evidence tier: [R] = named in a 2026/2025 review above; [K] = general recurring pattern from CBSE sample papers and board papers known to the analyst, NOT verified from a fetched source this session. Marks band is typical placement.

| Chapter | Recurring styles | Typical band | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Real Numbers | HCF/LCM word problem (bells, tracks, equal batches) [K]; HCF x LCM = product as AR [R 2026]; prove sqrt(p) or a + b sqrt(p) irrational [R 2026]; prime factorisation MCQ [K] | 1 (MCQ/AR), 3 (proof), sometimes 2 | Easy to moderate |
| Polynomials | zeros from graph; sum/product of zeros; form quadratic; find k [K] | 1, 2, 3 | Direct |
| Pair of Linear Eq | consistency condition and k [K]; word problems (speed, ages, fractions) [R 2026]; graph-based in Basic | 1, 3, 5 | Basic graph questions called hard in 2026 |
| Quadratic Eq | discriminant/nature of roots with k [K]; factorise/formula; word problem (speed-time, area) [R Basic 2026 "lengthy"] | 1, 2, 3, 5 | Highest chapter weight |
| AP | nth term/sum, number of terms [K]; case study [R 2026] | 1, 2, 3, 4 (case) | Reliable Section E candidate |
| Coordinate Geometry | distance formula (collinear, equidistant, shape) [K]; section formula, midpoint [K] | 1, 2, 3 | One 2-mark in 2025 Q25 reported hard |

| Circles | tangent lengths from external point [K]; radius-tangent angle; cyclic quadrilateral/circumscribed [R 2026] | 1, 2, 3, 5 | Tangent theorem questions hard in 2026 Std |
| Trig (intro, identities) | evaluate with standard values; prove identity [K] | 1, 3 | Straight formula |
| Heights & Distances | one or two angles, tower/building/kite [K]; case study [R 2026] | 3, 5, 4 (case) | Needs diagram |
| Areas Related to Circles | sector/segment, design or field region [K] | 1, 2, 3 | Basic 2026 questions "lengthy" |
| Surface Areas & Volumes | combined solids, cylinder+hemisphere [R 2026]; melt and recast [K]; case study [R 2026] | 1, 3, 5, 4 | Frequent 5-mark |

Case-study contexts: reported 2026 topics are AP, SAV and trig application (above). Typical contexts [K]: AP (savings, seating, steps), SAV (tank, toy, tent), coordinate/distance (maps, playground), height and distance (tower, flag), probability (games). Not verified against 2023-25 papers.

Difficulty trend (reviewer opinion): 2023 Std moderate, Basic easy-moderate (https://news.careers360.com/cbse-class-10-maths-exam-analysis-simple-direct-questions-easy-balanced-paper-student-reactions-here/amp via search summary); 2024 easy to moderate, case studies hardest, Basic simpler (Careers360 2024 link); 2025 easy to moderate, "a bit tougher than 2024", Set 3 harder (Careers360 2025 link); 2026 moderate, "easier than previous year" per one teacher, MCQs lengthy; Standard vs Basic reports conflict (Careers360 2026, prosyllabus).

## 5. 2026 vs 2025
- Structure identical (38 Q, 20/5/6/4/3 split).
- New: Phase 2 optional second exam; Phase 1 on 17 Feb (earlier than 10 March 2025).
- MCQs described as more lengthy/concept-based in 2026.
- 2026 skewed to PYQ-style items in C and D (circles/triangles theorems, irrationality, dice, mean/median/mode) per India TV; 2025 reports list no topics.
- Difficulty: 2025 "slightly tougher than 2024" vs 2026 "moderate, easier than last year" per some reviewers; reviewer opinion only.
- 2026 includes a two-unknown mean question and combined solid question in D; 2025 equivalents not published.

## 6. Confidence / gaps
- No official document read (certificate error on cbseacademic.nic.in). Unit marks, 38Q structure and 2026-27 "no change" rely on consistent secondary sources; the 50% competency number rests on news summaries.
- No chapter-by-chapter question counts for any year (2023-2026) were found; observed columns are null. To obtain them, download the official papers and tag questions manually.
- 2023 Section A: one source says 20 MCQ; AR introduction year (2024) is inferred.
- 2023 deletions list conflicts across blogs (e.g. "area of triangle" in coordinate geometry; Careers360 vs PW).
- Phase 2 Maths date: 15 May (Careers360, newsonair) vs 5 May (PW) ; end date 21 May vs 1 June.
- Standard vs Basic 2026 difficulty reports contradict each other.
- Whether Phase 2 papers are structurally identical: sources say "similar".
